About Us

Our journey of creating positive impact

Navi Disha Foundation is a non-governmental organization dedicated to creating sustainable and impactful change in communities across India. Founded with a vision to empower and uplift, we focus on addressing key societal challenges through innovative and inclusive approaches.

Our Vision

A society where everyone has equal opportunities to learn, grow, and thrive.

Our Mission

To implement sustainable solutions that create lasting positive change in communities.

Our Journey

Key milestones and accomplishments

2015

Foundation Established

Navi Disha Foundation was founded with a mission to create sustainable change in communities.

2017

First Education Initiative

Launched our first education program, reaching over 500 underprivileged children.

2019

Healthcare Expansion

Extended our services to include healthcare initiatives, establishing medical camps in 10 villages.

2021

Environmental Focus

Initiated environmental conservation programs with a successful reforestation campaign.

2023

Livelihood Program

Started vocational training centers, empowering communities with sustainable livelihood options.

Our Programs

Making a difference through targeted initiatives

Education Initiatives

Our education programs provide resources, mentorship, and support to underprivileged children, helping them access quality education and build a foundation for their future.

  • School enrollment drives
  • After-school support programs
  • Digital literacy workshops
  • Scholarship opportunities

Medical Services

Our healthcare initiatives aim to provide medical services to underserved communities, focusing on preventive care, health education, and access to essential treatments.

  • Health camps in rural areas
  • Maternal and child health programs
  • Health awareness workshops
  • Medical aid for critical cases

Environmental Conservation

Our environmental initiatives promote sustainable practices, community involvement in conservation, and awareness about climate change and its impact.

  • Tree plantation drives
  • Waste management programs
  • Clean water initiatives
  • Environmental education workshops

Livelihood Support

Our livelihood programs focus on skill development, entrepreneurship, and creating sustainable income opportunities for marginalized communities.

  • Vocational training programs
  • Micro-enterprise development
  • Financial literacy workshops
  • Job placement assistance
